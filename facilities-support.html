Facilities Operations Support

Your Reliable Back-Office Partner for UK Facilities Management

Facilities Management runs on more than dispatching engineers, it depends on keeping every job moving from first request to final sign-off. As maintenance volumes grow, admin becomes the bottleneck that slows response times and frustrates clients. We work as an extension of your office team, streamlining workflows and tightening coordination so you can maintain service excellence as you scale. Every solution is shaped around your existing processes and systems, never forced into a template.

Operational Coordination

We keep every job moving: request intake, job logging, priority & SLA management, engineer and contractor allocation, appointment scheduling, tenant & client communication, and work-order tracking with proactive follow-ups. Fewer delays, clearer visibility, and every stakeholder informed throughout the job lifecycle.

Engineer Recruitment & Contractor Onboarding

We build and maintain your network of qualified engineers, sourcing and screening self-employed and in-house candidates across Plumbing, Electrical, Gas, HVAC, Roofing, Drainage, Locksmith, Decorating, and Multi-Trade Maintenance. We verify qualifications and compliance documents, agree rates in line with your standards, create engineer profiles in your FM system, and keep your contractor database current as you grow.

Contractor & Compliance Management

Quality and accountability on every job: contractor coordination, quotation and approval tracking, follow-on works scheduling, completion reports, compliance certificates, photographic evidence, and Health & Safety documentation, fully documented and audit-ready before every job closes.

Reporting & Business Support

Job status and outstanding works tracking, SLA and contractor performance monitoring, operational dashboards, and maintenance history management, the numbers your team needs, without the admin overhead.

Financial Administration & Invoice Management

We process supplier and contractor invoices, match them against work orders and POs, verify supporting documentation, monitor approvals, track job costs, and prepare monthly financial and operational reports, keeping records accurate, organised, and audit-ready while your internal team carries less of the load.

Business Process Optimisation

No two FM businesses run the same way. We start by understanding yours, then improve workflow efficiency, office-to-engineer communication, contractor response times, job completion rates, documentation accuracy, and customer experience, solutions built around your objectives, not a one-size-fits-all fix.

Finance & Recovery

Property Accounting & Arrears Management

Property Accounting. We reconcile every transaction, rent, fees, expenses, contractor invoices, in real time and integrate directly with your property management software. At month's end, you receive detailed profit & loss statements, balance sheets, and customised landlord reports, all accessible via your secure dashboard.
